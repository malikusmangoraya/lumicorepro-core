## Fonts and imagery

The bundled user interface references web fonts served from Google Fonts and
photographic placeholders from Unsplash. Both are used under their respective
licences and are fetched at runtime; no font or image binary is redistributed
in this repository.

## Motion footage

The hero panel streams a royalty-free stock clip from Pexels (an abstract
data-network loop) with its poster photograph from Unsplash. Both are fetched
at runtime under their respective licences; no video or image binary is
redistributed in this repository.
