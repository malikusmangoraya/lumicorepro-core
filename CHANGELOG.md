| 2026-11-04 | 8.37 | **A generated product shipped an AI surface anyone could spend money on.** `ai_saas_kit` mounted `POST /api/v1/ai/generate`, `/chat` and `/files` with no auth, so every product built from it exposed the owner's provider key to whoever found the URL — the same kit that had just been fixed for the read-only-disk 404.
